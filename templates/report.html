Definitions & limitations

Churn rate

Churned customers divided by all customers within a segment. The dashboard reports associations in this dataset, not causal relationships.

Monthly charges

The sum of listed monthly charges for accounts marked as churned. It is a cohort-size indicator, not a forecast of recoverable revenue.

Source & scope

7,043 customer records from the Telco Customer Churn dataset. Segment percentages are rounded to one decimal place; groups across dimensions overlap.

Source: IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset on Kaggle. Project: Customer Churn Prediction on GitHub.
