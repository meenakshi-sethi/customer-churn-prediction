What stands out

Patterns in this dataset

  • Month-to-month customers have the highest contract-segment churn rate (42.7%).
  • Fiber optic customers have the highest internet-service churn rate (41.9%).
  • Electronic check customers have the highest payment-method churn rate (45.3%).
  • Customers who churned averaged 18.0 months of tenure, versus 37.6 months for retained customers.

These are descriptive associations, not causal effects. Segment memberships overlap across charts.

Retention priorities to test

Treat these as hypotheses; validate impact with controlled tests.

PRIORITY 01

Strengthen first-year retention

47.4% churn · 1,037 churned

The 0-12 months group accounts for 55.5% of all churned customers. Test onboarding and early-tenure support, then measure against a holdout.

PRIORITY 02

Review month-to-month offers

42.7% churn · 1,655 churned

Compare retention offers or contract options for this group. Its size makes it a high-volume segment, but results should be tested rather than assumed.

PRIORITY 03

Investigate electronic-check experience

45.3% churn · 1,071 churned

Check billing and payment friction for electronic-check customers before testing a payment-method intervention.

Reading the analysis

Definitions & limitations

Churn rate

Churned customers divided by all customers within a segment. The dashboard reports associations in this dataset, not causal relationships.

Monthly charges

The sum of listed monthly charges for accounts marked as churned. It is a cohort-size indicator, not a forecast of recoverable revenue.

Source & scope

7,043 customer records from the Telco Customer Churn dataset. Segment percentages are rounded to one decimal place; groups across dimensions overlap.

Source: IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset on Kaggle. Project: Customer Churn Prediction on GitHub.
